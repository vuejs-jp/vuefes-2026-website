Vue Fes Japan 2026 is calling for session speakers.

If you have technical knowledge, practical experiences, or ideas related to Vue.js that you'd like to share with the community, we encourage you to apply. Previous speaking experience is not required—first-time speakers are welcome!

- Topics related to the Vue.js ecosystem
- Ability to present in Japanese or English
- Availability to attend the event in October 2026
