At Vue Fes Japan 2026, we have created a special program to support and inspire students who will lead the future of our tech community.

It is a great opportunity to connect with other students who share your passion for front-end development. You can also enjoy talking with Vue.js community members, engineers at the heart of the OSS world, and professionals from our Student Support Sponsor companies.

The program features lightning talks by sponsor companies along with casual networking sessions where you can chat about technology and career paths. We hope this will be a chance for you to get real-world advice on learning and community involvement directly from experienced engineers.

We look forward to seeing your time at Vue Fes Japan 2026 turn into new insights and meaningful connections. See you there!

This event is part of the Student Support Program. Only students who have been accepted through the application process can participate.
