Got a question about frontend, development tools, or your career? Maybe there’s something you’ve been wondering about, but it feels too small to go out of your way to ask.

The “Ask Anything Board” is a Q&A board created by everyone at the event. Write down your questions or concerns on a sticky note and post them on the board—you might find that someone at the venue has an answer, some advice, or an experience to share.

Whether you’re asking a question, sharing an answer, or just stopping by to see what others are talking about, everyone is welcome! Let’s bring together the knowledge and experiences of the Vue Fes Japan community and enjoy new discoveries and connections.
