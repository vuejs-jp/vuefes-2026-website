### How to Purchase

There are 2 ways to purchase:

**Pre-order**

If you pre-order through STORES, you can pick up your items at the Vue Fes Japan 2026 venue on the day of the event. Please note that we do not ship to homes or other locations.

<br>

Pre-order period: Varies by product. Please check each product page on STORES for details.

Pick-up date and time: October 24, 2026 (Saturday), 9:00-18:00

Pick-up location: Vue Fes Store booth inside the Vue Fes Japan 2026 venue

Please show your order confirmation email when picking up your items.

Items will also be available at the Vue Fes Store booth inside the Vue Fes Japan 2026 venue on the day of the event. Quantities are limited, so we recommend pre-ordering to ensure your purchase.
