Japan's largest Vue.js conference, Vue Fes Japan, is back again this year!

With Vapor mode just around the corner, Vue.js has solidified its core foundation and entered a phase of deeper evolution. New tools such as Vize, Verter, and Golar have also emerged, bringing fresh momentum to the ecosystem.

Vite, created by Vue.js author Evan You, has transformed the web development experience. Through VoidZero, the company he founded, he is driving further innovation in the JavaScript ecosystem by developing a unified toolchain powered by Oxc at its core, including Rolldown, Oxlint, and Oxfmt.
At Vue.js Amsterdam in March 2026, two new products were unveiled: Vite+ and Void These releases further expand their impact on the JavaScript community.

In response to this rapidly evolving landscape, we rebranded Vue Fes Japan last year with the goal of creating a conference that goes beyond Vue.js itself. We also made significant efforts to make the event accessible to a global audience, welcoming more than 800 participants.

At Vue Fes Japan 2026, prominent OSS developers from across the JavaScript community, including Evan You himself, will come together. Through sessions, panel discussions, and various events, you can gain cutting-edge insights from the global frontier while building real connections across communities in person. That is what makes Vue Fes Japan truly special.

Come join us and experience the excitement and inspiration firsthand!
