Have you ever used a frontend framework like Vue.js or React and wondered, “What’s actually happening under the hood?”

In this hands-on workshop, we’ll explore how frontend frameworks work using chibivue, a learning resource designed to help you understand the internals of Vue.js.

By implementing key concepts such as reactivity and the Virtual DOM yourself, you’ll get a firsthand look at what’s usually hidden behind the libraries and frameworks you use every day. Through this experience, you’ll gain a deeper understanding of how frontend frameworks work under the hood.

Whether you’re a Vue.js user or work with another framework such as React, there’s something here for everyone. Once you understand how things work behind the scenes, the frameworks you use every day may start to feel a little more familiar—and a lot more interesting.
